01

Your content dies after upload

You publish a great 30-minute video that took days to produce. It gets views on your channel, then the algorithm moves on. The best moments inside never surface again.

02

Repurposing is a full-time job

Clipping, editing, captioning, scheduling across platforms — it requires dedicated staff. Most creators can't afford the time or the team for consistent short-form output.

03

One account limits your reach

Posting clips on your own account means you're competing with your own content. Fan pages and secondary channels multiply touchpoints without cannibalizing your main presence.

04

Distribution costs too much

Agencies that offer multi-platform distribution charge premium rates that only top-tier creators can afford. Mid-tier creators are left without scalable solutions.
