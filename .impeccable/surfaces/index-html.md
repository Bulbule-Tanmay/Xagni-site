# Surface Brief: index.html

- **Scope:** Full marketing & intake landing page (`index.html`) for Xagni.
- **Visitor Mode:** Persuade
- **Primary Audience & Job:** Indian long-form creators evaluating short-form multi-platform distribution (IG Reels, YT Shorts, FB Reels) and independent clippers applying to operate fan pages.
- **Primary Actions:** Launch a creator distribution campaign (`Basic ₹7,000`, `Growth ₹10,000`, `Premium ₹20,000`), inspect the 4-stage pipeline, scan or send the campaign brief to `hello@clipforge.in`, or apply as an independent clipper.
- **Memorable Moment:** Full-bleed Sky Wash (`#dceeff`) poster opening under a crisp black scrolling marquee where crushed `800`-weight condensed uppercase display blocks (*"ONE VIDEO. 75 PAGES."*) sit physically in front of a grainy 3D inflatable Electric Blue (`#4da2ff`) tubular ribbon, surrounded by 1px black-outlined rotated sticker cut-outs in Ember, Sunburst, Mint Pop, and Voltage Violet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Xagni treats short-form multi-page distribution as a tactile inflatable sticker-book poster rather than a muted SaaS dashboard or quiet editorial monograph—refusing subtle gray cards, gradients, and drop shadows in favor of crushed sculptural display blocks, 1px hand-cut black outlines, and full-bleed pastel color bands.

OWN-WORLD: Slush inflatable sticker universe on pastel paper—alternating full-bleed bands across Sky Wash (`#dceeff`), Paper White (`#ffffff`), and Concrete Gray (`#cccccc`); a six-color shared sticker palette (`#4da2ff` Electric Blue 3D ribbons & washes, `#55db9c` Mint Pop, `#e9ccff` Lavender, `#fb4903` Ember, `#ffd731` Sunburst, `#5c4ade` Voltage Violet); `1px solid #000000` borders on every card, sticker, input, pill nav, and button; zero `box-shadow` and zero gradients; `Barlow Condensed` / `Antonio` `800`-weight display headlines at `0.75–0.80` line-height paired with `Inter` `500`/`700` UI copy (`"ss01", "tnum"`, `0.032em` uppercase label tracking); `1600px` pill controls and `20px–40px` soft-cornered cards.

STORY: Visitors immediately see that one long-form upload powers up to 75 fan pages and 250 weekly reels across Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook; explore the 4-stage clipping-to-publishing pipeline; compare transparent ₹7,000–₹20,000 monthly plans; see real creator quotes and clipper ownership terms; and submit or copy a tailored campaign or clipper brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed `#000000` top marquee strip with scrolling white `12px` `0.032em` uppercase copy, above a `#dceeff` Sky Wash hero poster featuring a top bar (`X` circular badge + `Xagni` left, `1600px` pill nav links + `+` trigger center, `#000000` filled CTA right), centered crushed `800`-weight display block (`ONE VIDEO. 75 PAGES.`) layered over a 3D grainy Electric Blue (`#4da2ff`) inflatable ribbon, four rotated `1px`-outlined sticker cut-outs (`#fb4903`, `#ffd731`, `#55db9c`, `#5c4ade`), concise subhead, and matched black-filled + white-ghost `1600px` pill CTAs.
