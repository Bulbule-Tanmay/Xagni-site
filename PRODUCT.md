# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (single-file `index.html`)

## Users

1. **Primary — Indian long-form creators** (podcasters, YouTubers, educators, streamers) who spend hours producing long-form videos and want consistent short-form reach without hiring a full-time editing team or risking channel cannibalization.
2. **Secondary — Independent clippers & fan-page operators** across India who want to build and own fan pages, claim clipping campaigns, earn variable pay per approved clip, and retain 100% of their platform monetization revenue.

## Product Purpose

Xagni is an affordable short-form content distribution agency for Indian creators. It turns long-form videos into edited, captioned, hook-driven vertical reels and distributes them simultaneously across an operator-owned network of fan pages on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels.

## Positioning

Unlike traditional editing agencies that only deliver video files back to a single creator channel, Xagni operates an end-to-end clipping and distribution pipeline: AI-assisted moment selection + human finishing + coordinated publishing across up to 75 independent fan pages (up to 250 weekly / 1,000 monthly reel placements) starting at ₹7,000/month.

## Operating Context

- Creators submit a long-form channel or sample video link, select a tier (Basic ₹7,000/mo, Growth ₹10,000/mo, or Premium ₹20,000/mo), and optionally approve clips prior to publication.
- Independent clippers own their fan pages (clearly labeled as unofficial fan pages), select matching campaigns, edit with Xagni tools and style guides, and earn per approved clip plus platform monetization.
- Both creator campaign inquiries and clipper applications route to `hello@clipforge.in` via email client launch or clipboard brief copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Plans & Scale:**
  - **Basic (₹7,000/month):** 5 fan pages per platform, 15 social accounts total, 10 reels/page/week, IG/YT/FB, captions & hooks, monthly report.
  - **Growth (₹10,000/month, Most Popular):** 10 fan pages per platform, 30 social accounts total, 10 reels/page/week, IG/YT/FB, full editing + B-roll + effects, weekly reports, content strategy guidance.
  - **Premium (₹20,000/month):** 25 fan pages per platform, 75 social accounts total, 10 reels/page/week, IG/YT/FB, priority editing pipeline, dedicated account manager, custom analytics dashboard.
- **Platforms:** Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels only.
- **Technical Constraint:** Single self-contained `index.html` with no build step, accessible keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and working email/clipboard intake flows.

## Product Principles

1. **Distribution is the product, not just editing** — every section proves multi-page reach, weekly placement volume, and platform coverage rather than generic video editing claims.
2. **Transparent unit economics** — Indian creators evaluate ROI immediately in ₹/month vs. accounts and weekly reel volume.
3. **Two-sided clarity** — creators and clippers each have an unmistakable path from discovery to the intake brief without friction.
4. **Honest mechanics** — fan-page ownership, optional client approval, and multi-page clip reuse are stated plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable tabs/accordions/stage controls, WCAG AA contrast across all text and controls, visible focus states, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
